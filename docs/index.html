Audiobook Library About

A free, growing collection on one restless question: what it means to stay human as our machines grow more capable. It begins where the question grabbed us — a 2026 letter on AI and the human person — and follows the thread outward, across the thinkers, scriptures, and science fiction that have circled the same ground since antiquity. You don't have to believe any of it to be pulled in; come curious.

Narrated locally, with read-along text and a companion in the margins — no account, no tracking, no paywall.
